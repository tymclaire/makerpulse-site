Why MakerPulse

Everything you check, in one glance.

Stop opening three apps before you head out. MakerPulse puts the time, the weather and your next bus on your desk.

Features

A touchscreen with multiple features.

Time, weather, bus arrivals and a focus timer, all on one touchscreen. Tap between screens and make it yours.

  1. 1

    Clock and focus timer

    Time at a glance. Tap the Timer tile, pick a time and it starts.

  2. 2

    Weather

    Temperature, rain, wind, UV index, sunrise and sunset.

  3. 3

    Air and sky

    Singapore's PSI haze index and the KP-index.

  4. 4

    Bus arrivals

    Live arrival times for your stops in Singapore.

  5. 5

    Make it yours

    A settings page over WiFi: nickname, six screen colours, brightness, night mode, timer presets and location.

  6. 6

    Open and hackable

    Open-source firmware, made for The Young Maker workshops.

Four screens, one tap apart.
